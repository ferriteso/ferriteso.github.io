Named for the material

Iron oxide,
fired hard.

Ferrite is a ceramic made of iron oxide and a second metal, pressed and sintered until it is hard and brittle. It sits inside nearly every power supply and radio you own, shaping magnetic fields without wasting energy as heat. Nobody sees it. Everything depends on it. That is the job we want.

Resistivity, NiZn105–107 Ω·mBlocks eddy currents, so cores stay cool at high frequency.
Sintering~1300 °CFired once, then it holds its shape for decades.
Hardness5–6 MohsHarder than steel tools can easily cut. Ground, never machined.
StructureSpinelA cubic oxide lattice. Small, repeated, very stable.
